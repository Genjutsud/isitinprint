    How a status is decided

    Official
    The publisher posted it on its own site or official social account: a reprint, a restock, an end-of-print notice, or a reprint policy that rules the set out.
    Current release
    The publisher released the set within the last 12 months and hasn't posted an end-of-print notice.
    Historical record
    The set's publisher no longer makes the game, so the set can't be printed again in that form.
    No official word
    The publisher hasn't said anything. Retailer listings, rumors and influencer reports never change a status here.

    Statuses change only when The Pokémon Company, Wizards of the Coast, Ravensburger, Bandai or Konami post news. "Last known print" is the most recent print run the publisher confirmed, or the year printing ended.
